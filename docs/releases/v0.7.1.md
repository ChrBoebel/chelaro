# Chelaro v0.7.1 — A quieter workspace

The desktop breadcrumb bar is removed, leaving more vertical space for conversations.
Theme switching and available updates now live at the bottom of the sidebar. The Chelaro
logo doubles in size, with a larger wordmark. Repetitive chat and sidebar hints are removed.

Mobile navigation retains its opening button, and update dialogs appear above the sidebar
with independent keyboard focus handling. Financial proposal decisions and data are unchanged.
